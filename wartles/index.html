How a war goes

Dig. Build. Shell up.

  1. Dig shardsYour Diggers work the island's shard seams; the Tidecallers mine the sunken reef too. Shards buy your works and your shells.
  2. Raise your worksStables, forges and watchtowers. Open water is a wall: tortoises arch it in stone, pond turtles raft it with reeds, sea turtles swim, and anyone can wade a ford at low tide.
  3. Send in the shellsBiters at the front, boulders over the top, and carriers that float a platoon across the sea. Drag a box round them, then right-click (or tap, on a phone) where they should go.
  4. Call down a specialWar drums, a rockslide and more, won in the campaign and called down where you point. And each people has its own great power: a stone that stuns, a poison bloom, a flood.

Earn Mastery with a people, raise its titan's hall, and wake it, one at a time. Each titan is a camp that walks.

Questions

Before you march

Is it really free?

Yes. Every people, every mission and every way to fight, free in your browser.

Do I need to play CLI Tortoises first?

No. This link opens the war table straight away. The table stands in the Elder's camp on Shellhaven, in CLI Tortoises: leave the table, and you're on the island.

Will it run on my phone?

Yes. The war table has its own phone layout, and the game picks graphics to suit your device, stepping them down if it can't keep up.

Where does my progress go?

It's kept in this browser: the missions you've won, your Mastery with each people, and what it's woken.

Can I fight my friends?

Not yet. For now you fight the table's own commanders, on Easy, Normal or Hard.

05The story

Shellhaven and the sea

Questions

Before you set off

Is it really free?

Yes. Both chapters and every way to play, right here in your browser.

Do I need a crypto wallet?

No. The tortoises are a collection on the Chia blockchain, but you don't need a wallet or a token to play. Linking a wallet is optional, and only for collectors.

Where does my progress go?

It saves in this browser as you play, and picks up where you left off. Make a free account to keep it safe on another device and to show up on the leaderboards.

Will it run on my phone?

Yes. It's built to play with one thumb, and it picks its graphics to suit your device. A computer with a keyboard works just as well.

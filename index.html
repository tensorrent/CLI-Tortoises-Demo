The world

A sea of turtle islands

Shellhaven is home: a camp, a barn of sleeping tortoises, an Elder by the fire. Past its beaches the sea is full of islands, and every one of them is a turtle.

Chapter I

Shellhaven and the sea

Wake the stable, commune with the Echo Stones, and sail out among the islands round Shellhaven. Out past them all sleeps Old Deepbreath, the oldest turtle in the sea. Give her stones their rhythm, and she wakes.

Chapter II

The Ebblands

She carries you across on her back. When she breathes out, the sea goes down and a land comes up out of it: a blank map, a drowned harbour, the Wreck on the Hill and the Old Beacon.

Ways to play

Ride it, battle it, deal it, command it

Battle and tame

Wild tortoise battles

Your tortoise's moves are cards, a fresh hand every turn. Wear a wild one down and win it over, and it joins your stable and follows you about.

Cards

The Shell Duel

A card game at the table with your stable as the deck: tortoises, snacks and Echo Scrolls. Knock out your rival's side before they knock out yours.

Strategy

Wartles & Wartioses

Lead a people of turtles on a war table: dig shards, raise an army, fend off the Clatter's crab raids. A campaign of fifteen missions, or a skirmish.

The herd

10,000 tortoises. No two alike.

Every tortoise in the game is one of the CLI Tortoises: its own shell, print, skin, hat, neckwear and shoes. You'll meet them roaming the islands, battle them, and ride the ones you tame.

The Scroll

Its mind is written by what you do

  • Every tortoise carries a small mind, seeded from its token: how bold, wary and curious it starts out.
  • Your choices drift it. Spare a foe or press the attack, help a stranger or walk on: each one is sealed into its Scroll.
  • The Scroll only grows, and it replays to the same root anywhere, so its history is provably its own.
  • The Elder reads it, and your tortoise grows into a Champion, a Guardian, a Seeker, a Hermit, a Renegade or a Wanderer.

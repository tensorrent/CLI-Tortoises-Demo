What's inside

One island, four ways to play

It's the whole game, running in your browser. Every NFT tortoise has its own traits (shell, skin, hat, scarf, shoes), and you'll meet them roaming, fight them and ride them.

Explore

Ride the island

Walk, sprint and slide in your shell across Shellhaven. Talk to Elder Mossback, trade with the giant chicken merchants, dive for wrecks, and take on quests for the island's people.

Battle and tame

Wild tortoise battles

Your tortoise's moves are cards, and you draw a fresh hand every turn. Wear a wild one down and win it over, and it joins your stable and follows you around the island.

Cards

The Shell Duel

A card game at the table, with your stable as the deck. Play tortoises, snacks and Echo Scrolls, and knock out your rival's side before they knock out yours.

Strategy

Wartles & Wartioses

Lead the Hearthshell Legion, the Mire Pact or the Tidecallers on a war table: dig shards, raise an army, fend off the Clatter's crab raids, and play the campaign or a skirmish.

The Scroll

Its mind is written by what you do

  • Every tortoise carries a small opcode mind, seeded from its token: how bold, wary and curious it is from the start.
  • Your choices drift it. Spare a foe or press the attack, help a stranger or walk on: each one is sealed into its Scroll.
  • The Scroll only grows, and it replays to the same root anywhere, so its history is provably its own.
  • The Elder reads it, the story is told from it, and your tortoise grows into a Champion, a Guardian, a Seeker, a Hermit, a Renegade or a Wanderer.

How to play

Made for one thumb, fine with a keyboard

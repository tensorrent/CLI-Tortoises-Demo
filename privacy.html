Privacy

Last updated 20 November 2026.

Who runs this site

CLI Tortoises and this demo are run by KOBA42 / TensorRent. To get in touch, use the contact details at koba42.com.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub logs the IP address of each visitor and the pages asked for, for security and to keep the service running. GitHub handles those logs under its own privacy statement. We don't get or keep a copy.

What the game keeps, and where

  • Your save and your settings stay in this browser, in its local storage, under names that start cli-tortoises. (and two small panel settings that start tt-). A rig you drop on the Rig Lab is kept in this browser's storage too.
  • None of it is sent to us. There are no accounts in this demo.
  • To remove it, clear this site's data in your browser's settings. That also wipes your save.

Clips

A clip of your game is recorded on your device and is never uploaded. It only leaves your device if you share it yourself. The link a clip's end card spells out is a short link at is.gd, a third-party link service that forwards to this site.

Voices

The characters' recorded voices are synthetic, and the recordings are files on this site. Where a line has no recording, your browser may read it aloud with its own speech voices; some browsers' online voices send that text to the browser's maker.

Links to other sites

The X (Twitter) link and your device's share button lead to third parties, which have their own privacy rules.

What this site doesn't do

No analytics, no cookies, no ads and no tracking. The fonts are served from this site, not from a font service.
